# Privacy Policy — Support Bot (test phase)

*Last updated: 21 September 2026*

## Who we are
Support Bot is a WhatsApp support service in a closed test. It is operated by Nimrod Zin, Tel Aviv, Israel.
Contact: nimrod.zin+support-bot@gmail.com

## Who can use it
During the test, only people who have been invited can message the service.

## What we receive
- Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
- The messages and files you choose to send us.

We do not access anything else on your phone or in your WhatsApp account.

## How we use it
Only to reply to you and to find and fix problems with the service. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send you marketing.

## Who else handles it
- **Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)** delivers messages between you and us.
- **Cloudflare** carries traffic to our server during the test.

Before we add automatic answers or bug reporting, this page will be updated to name any further services involved (for example an AI provider or a private issue tracker).

## Logs
Our server logs record which events happened and a partly hidden phone number. They never contain the text of your messages.

## How long we keep it
Test data is deleted within 30 days. At present, conversations are held only in the server's memory and are lost whenever it restarts.

## Your choices
Email us at the address above to ask what we hold about you, to have it deleted, or to stop using the service.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
